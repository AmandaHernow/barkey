BarKey · Amanda Hernow
Free · Offline · Open source

Scan into the field you’re already using.

BarKey is an Android keyboard that uses your device’s rear camera to scan barcodes and QR codes, then inserts the decoded value into the focused text field. Scanning and decoding happen locally on your device.
