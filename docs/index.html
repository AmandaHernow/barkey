Scan into the field you’re already using.

BarKey is an Android keyboard that uses your device’s rear camera to scan barcodes and QR codes, then inserts the decoded value into the focused text field. Scanning and decoding happen locally on your device.

A camera scanner where you type

Enable BarKey in Android settings, select it from the keyboard picker, focus a text field, and scan. BarKey decodes each camera frame on the device and inserts the result at the active cursor through Android’s input method connection. It does not use the clipboard or keep a permanent scan history.

BarKey is free software with no paid features, advertisements, accounts, or user tracking. Read more about the project, its open-source libraries, and its developer on the About page.
